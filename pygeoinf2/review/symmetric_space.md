# Review: `pygeoinf2/symmetric_space` against v1 `pygeoinf/symmetric_space`

> **Note (2026-08-27):** the decisions recorded in `pygeoinf2/REVIEW.md` §11 (D-1 … D-13) override the Must/Should/Consider ranking below wherever they conflict — in particular D-1 (sphere vectors are `SHGrid`, `sampling=1` default), D-2 (points in `(lat, lon)` degrees), D-3 (per-geometry submodules with `Lebesgue`/`Sobolev` subclasses), D-4 (`from_matrix(..., form=)`), D-6 (parallel hooks around operators), D-12 (path *integral* operator), D-13 (convex solvers restored).

Method: read all of v1 (`symmetric_space.py`, `sphere.py`, `circle.py`, `line.py`, `plane.py`, `torus.py`, `datasets.py`) and all of v2 (`base.py`, `fourier.py`, `box.py`, `sphere.py`, `plotting/`, `data/`), plus DESIGN.md §3.5, 12, 13, 19, 20, 21.x and the catalogue. Claims were checked by running throwaway scripts. pyshtools 4.14.1, finufft and cartopy are all installed, so every path was exercised; 16-core machine. No code was modified.

Headline: the core numerics of v2 are sound (orthonormality, radius scaling, packing for odd/rectangular/3D grids, the double-Fourier-sphere NUFFT and its adjoint all verify to ~1e-11 or better), and v2 fixes a real v1 sign bug. But the catalogue materially overstates what was ported: **every geometric/observation method exists only on `Sphere`** (the periodic box and `Box`/`Interval` have no `geodesic_distance`, so `path_average_operator`, `geodesic_ball_average_operator` and `covariance_function` all raise on them), `with_degree`/`degree_transfer_operator`/coefficient operators are sphere-only, `to_coefficients`/`from_coefficients` are gone, the Sobolev-order guard on point evaluation is gone, `Box.project_function` silently replaced v1's raised-cosine taper with a hard cutoff, and the sphere's NUFFT forward path is slower than v1 at realistic point counts because of finufft's default threading.

### 1.2 Retained and extended (verified)
Transform round trip (6.8e-14 at lmax=128); Sobolev as diagonal metric (no mass-weighted lift needed); NUFFT on boxes (1–3D) and on the sphere via double Fourier sphere; `spherical_cap_integral`/`average` exact (cap of 1 on radius 2 matches `2πR²(1−cos α)` to 1e-15; agrees with 400-node quadrature to 5e-5); `geodesic_quadrature` weights sum to arc length; `degree_transfer_operator` adjoint derived (correct under differing metrics); `pointwise_variance`, `pointwise_variance_at`, `power_measure`, `correlated_measure`; `derivative_operator` on any axis (D sin = cos to 1e-14; adjoint identity on `H²` to 1e-12); `interior_mask`, `support_projection`; `walk_from`; real station/event tables shipped.

## 2. Algorithmic performance (measured, lmax=128 unless stated)

**Transforms.** `to_components`/`from_components`: v1 DH 2.25/2.39 ms, v1 DH2 2.89/2.75 ms, v2 2.66/2.74 ms. Same cost; v2 grids hold 2× the points of v1's default, so field memory and every pointwise operation are 2×. `SHExpandDH(..., sampling=2, lmax_calc=lmax)` and `MakeGridDH(..., sampling=2, lmax=lmax)` (`sphere.py:241-267`) are the right calls; nothing is over-resolved beyond that. Packing in `fourier.py` is fully vectorised (`_FourierPacking.__init__`, `to_components`, `from_components`), unlike v1's per-mode `add_mode` loop — an improvement.

**`Sphere.evaluate` NUFFT path (`sphere.py:457-494`) is slower than v1 at moderate n, and the cause is finufft's default `nthreads=0` (all 16 cores).** cProfile: 160 ms of a 170–200 ms call is inside `finufft execute`; with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` the same call is 20 ms. First-run measurements were worse (794 ms at n=2000). Numbers:

| n | v1 forward (`SHCoeffs.expand`, Fortran) | v2 forward (default threads) | v2 forward (1 thread) | v1 adjoint | v2 adjoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 000 | 93 ms | 173–794 ms | 20 ms | 144 ms | 33–232 ms |
| 20 000 | 888 ms | 200–418 ms | ~25 ms | 1 133 ms | 30–92 ms |

So the DESIGN §21.12 table (26 ms) is reproducible only with threading controlled. `path_average_operator` (200 paths × 20 nodes): v2 forward 189 ms vs v1 149 ms; adjoint 31 ms vs 232 ms. Nothing in the API lets the caller set `nthreads` or `eps` on the operator (`point_evaluation_operator` at `base.py:691` has no pass-through), and finufft threads will also oversubscribe inside any outer `joblib` loop. The direct (basis-matrix) route at n=2000 is 305 ms vs v1's Fortran scattered evaluation at 73 ms; the crossover thresholds (`_TRANSFORM_MIN_POINTS = 512`, `sphere.py:47`) were presumably tuned under the same threading artefact.

**`Sphere._quadrature` (`sphere.py:358-382`)** runs `2(lmax+1)` full `SHExpandDH` calls on first NUFFT use: 0.09 s (lmax 64), 0.8–1.1 s (128), **9.9 s (256)**, extrapolating to ~2 min at 512. `pyshtools.utils.DHaj(n)` returns the same weights to a relative 1.5e-16 (checked), so the bootstrap is unnecessary. DESIGN §21.12 says `DHaj` "is not exposed by the installed pyshtools" — it is.

**Per-instance caches are rebuilt by `with_order`.** `_packing` (Python loop of length dim, `sphere.py:187-196`), `_legendre_indices` (Python loop calling `PlmIndex` per component, `sphere.py:219-227`; closed form is `l(l+1)/2 + m`) and `_quadrature` are `cached_property` on the *instance*. `multiplication_operator`, `flexural_operator`, `l2_products_operator`, `derivative_operator` all call `self.with_order(0.0)` (`base.py:206, 781, 870`), creating a fresh `Sphere` each time: 0.22 s per call at lmax=256 before any work, and the 10 s `_quadrature` would be paid again if the base space evaluates. `Sphere(512)` construction + `_legendre_indices` is 1.06 s.

**Transform counts per application (sphere, lmax=32; v1 in brackets).** `multiply` 2 (0); `gradient_dot_product` 12 (6); `multiplication_operator` on Sobolev: value 6 (0), adjoint 6 (4); `order_inclusion_operator` value 4 (0); `flexural_operator` value 54–58 (26), adjoint 58 (30). Two causes: (i) `multiply` truncates (deliberate, DESIGN 21.7), and (ii) `lift_formal_adjoint` (`base.py:1250-1262`) round-trips `from_components(to_components(x))` on both value and adjoint even when domain and base share the same grid and `multiply` has already truncated — 4 transforms per application doing nothing. `lift_formal_adjoint` itself is O(1) gram solves (pointwise divides), which is fine. `DiagonalLinearOperator._value` is 2 transforms (`diagonal.py:_value`), same as v1.

**`power_measure` (`base.py:585`)** computes `counts` with a list comprehension that is O(dim²): 0.04 s at lmax 128, 0.43 s at 255, ~7 s at 511. `np.bincount(degrees)[degrees]` is O(dim).

**`pairs_within_distance` (`sphere.py:1013`)** allocates `(n, n, 3)` doubles: 576 MB and 0.21 s at n=3000 versus v1's `cKDTree` at 7 MB and 0.08 s. At n=10⁴ this is 2.4 GB. (Side note: v1 double-counts the diagonal under current scipy — 28 474 vs 25 474 pairs — which would double diagonal entries when the COO is summed into a sparse matrix. v2's count is right.)

**`cluster_points`** is O(n·k) with numpy; fine. **`project_function`** on the sphere is a Python loop over 8(lmax+1)² points (61 ms vs v1 13 ms at lmax=128; v1's loop is cheaper per point). **`basis_at`** 0.28 ms per call at lmax=128 — the earlier quadratic bug is fixed. **Dense `point_evaluation_operator(dense=True)`** builds 2000 rows in 207 ms, unchunked (fine, opt-in). `path_average_operator`'s `W E` factorisation stores only the node list and a CSR of weights — memory is O(nodes), not an issue.

**Import time** `import pygeoinf2`: 0.27 s, dominated by scipy.linalg; pyshtools, finufft, matplotlib, cartopy are not imported until used. Good.

## 3. Code practice and quality

- **Hierarchy.** `SymmetricSpace(ArrayVectorMixin, HilbertModule, DiagonalMetricSpace)` → `PeriodicBox` → `Box`; `Sphere`. Cleaner than v1's ten classes, and folding Sobolev into a metric is mathematically right. But `SymmetricSpace` declares `geodesic_distance`, `geodesic_quadrature`, `walk_from`, `project_function`, `random_point`, `reference_point`, `degrees`, `gaussian_curvature` as *non-abstract* methods that raise `NotImplementedError` (`base.py:154, 263, 639, 791, 998, 1004, 1017, 1132, 1138`), so a subclass that forgets them still constructs and fails only when a downstream method is called — which is exactly how the box regressions went unnoticed. Either make them `@abstractmethod` or implement them on `PeriodicBox` (all are trivial there: Euclidean distance with periodic wrap, straight-line Gauss-Legendre nodes).
- **Lebesgue/Sobolev as one class with `order`.** Reasonable, and `Lebesgue(...)`/`Sobolev(lmax, order, length_scale)` factory functions (`sphere.py:1117-1131`, `fourier.py:568-589`) preserve the v1 call shape. Two frictions: the factories return a `Sphere`/`PeriodicBox`, so `isinstance(X, Sobolev)` no longer works and `type(X).__name__` says nothing about the metric; and `scale` was renamed `length_scale` because `scale` is the vector-scaling method — the docstring says so, but every v1 script passing `scale=` breaks.
- **Conventions.** `(colatitude, longitude)` radians is internally consistent and pinned by test, but it inverts v1 (`(lat, lon)` degrees), `spherical_cap_integral` takes an *angular* radius while `geodesic_ball_average_operator` takes a *physical* one (`sphere.py:737, 788`), and the data loaders convert from degrees privately. A public `Sphere.from_latlon_degrees(...)`/`to_latlon_degrees(...)` pair (or a `Point` helper) is needed for any downstream code.
- **Duplication.** `path_average_operator` and `geodesic_ball_average_operator` (`base.py:1032-1130`) are the same 45 lines twice. `Box.with_order`/`PeriodicBox.with_order`/`Sphere.with_order` are copy-paste. `_angles` exists in both `fourier.py:385` and `sphere.py:432`.
- **Dead code / errors.** `Box.support_projection` computes `traits` then unconditionally raises on a Sobolev space (`box.py:218-230`); the `traits` branch is unreachable. `pointwise_variance_at` builds `operator` that the exact path never uses (`base.py:624-632`). `Sphere.walk_from` returns points with colatitude > π for distances > πR (`sphere.py:571-582`); harmless for the covariance function but wrong as a geometric primitive.
- **Private-member reach-through.** `Sphere.degree_transfer_operator` reads `target._packing[1]` (`sphere.py:1047`); `plotting/sphere.py` and tests reach into `_quadrature`, `_double`, `_packing`. `power_measure`/`spectral_projection_operator` work only where `degrees` is overridden; on a base subclass they raise late.
- **Error handling.** Good input validation on constructors and points. Missing: no guard on `order <= dim/2` for Dirac/point evaluation (was `safe=True` in v1); `Sphere.geodesic_ball_quadrature` accepts `radius` up to `πR` but `spherical_cap_integral` up to `π` with no cross-check; `_read_table` re-parses the CSV on every `stations()` call.
- **Type hints.** Points are `Any` throughout; `basis_at(point: Any)`. Acceptable but a `Point = np.ndarray` alias plus a documented shape would help.
- **Optional dependencies.** Handled well: `_require_pyshtools` (`sphere.py:54`), finufft import inside `_nufft_layout`/`_use_transform`, cartopy in `_require_cartopy`. `PeriodicBox.evaluate` does `import finufft` *before* checking the layout (`fourier.py:411-415`), so without finufft it raises `ImportError` instead of falling back as the docstring promises; same in `accumulate` (`fourier.py:452`).
- **Threading.** No `nthreads`/`eps` control at operator level; finufft defaults to all cores (see §2).

## 4. Documentation gaps (concrete)

Docstrings are unusually good on *why*; they are weak on *what* (arguments, units, shapes). Public methods with two or more parameters and no `Args` section:

- `base.py:127 spectral_operator`, `:162 spectral_projection_operator` (what `lmin/lmax` mean on a box), `:212 estimate_truncation_degree` (does not say result ≤ current lmax), `:233 sobolev_symbol`, `:309 invariant_measure`, `:375 sobolev_measure`, `:400 heat_measure` (units of `time`), `:588 covariance_function` (units of `distances`; sphere-only), `:601 pointwise_variance_at`, `:639 walk_from`, `:665 evaluate`, `:677 accumulate`, `:691 point_evaluation_operator` (no `Returns`, no point convention, no note that it is meaningless when `order <= dim/2`), `:752 multiply` (aliasing/truncation cost), `:795 gradient_dot_product`, `:998-1026 geodesic_*` (units of `radius`, point types), `:1085 geodesic_ball_average_operator` (`radius` is physical, not angular).
- `fourier.py:400 evaluate`/`:438 accumulate` (`eps` meaning; fallback), `:495 pointwise_multiply` (how it differs from `multiply`), `:556 with_order`, `:568 Lebesgue`, `:575 Sobolev` (unit of `lengths`, that points are physical coordinates in `[0, L)`).
- `box.py:157 with_order`; `box.py:190 project_function` should state that the padding is *zero-filled with no taper* and what that does to a non-vanishing boundary value.
- `sphere.py:496 accumulate`, `:571 walk_from` (distances ≤ πR), `:584 random_points`, `:633-731 geodesic_*` (units), `:737 spherical_cap_integral`/`:782 spherical_cap_average` (`angular_radius` in radians on the unit sphere vs `radius` elsewhere; centre convention), `:832 coefficient_operator` (the returned "coefficients" are `R ×` pyshtools' orthonormal ones and are in the private packing order — say the order), `:869 stations`/`:883 earthquakes` (return type, that names are dropped), `:901 domain_mask` (`resolution` values; returns a grid array not a functional), `:937 cluster_points` (greedy, not v1's linkage), `:963 source_receiver_paths`, `:991 pairs_within_distance` (O(n²) memory), `:1064 with_order`, `:1117 Lebesgue`, `:1122 Sobolev`.
- Module-level: `sphere.py:17-21` says CS phase "excluded" — consistent with code (`csphase=1`) — but DESIGN §13.4 says "with the Condon-Shortley phase"; the design doc is wrong. `base.py` module docstring cites "DESIGN.md 5.6/13" rather than stating the conventions in-line.
- `pygeoinf2/data/README.md` gives no date, query, or licence for the USGS/IRIS tables.

## 5. Correctness concerns

Verified correct (no action): orthonormality with `radius ≠ 1` and with arbitrary box lengths, odd axis lengths, 3D; Nyquist handling in `_FourierPacking` (parity-aware fixed points; `test_a_nyquist_leading_mode_survives_the_round_trip`); the NUFFT spectrum widened by 2 per axis to avoid the Nyquist fold (`fourier.py:365`); DFS extension is a trigonometric polynomial (checked in tests and by 3.5e-11 agreement with the direct sum); the sphere NUFFT adjoint uses the *transpose* (isign=+1 type-1 then `fft2`), which is right because the end-to-end map is real; adjoint identity 1e-12; `spherical_cap_integral` scaling on `radius=2` (cap of 1 exact, average vs quadrature 5e-5); `degree_transfer_operator` adjoint carries `G_target`; `lift_formal_adjoint` metric ratios (`base.py:1247-1248`) match `M_X^{-1} A^{*U} M_Y`; `pointwise_variance` divides by `g_k` (the metric bug the design records).

**`gradient_dot_product` sign — v2 is right, v1 is wrong, verified independently.** With the positive Laplacian `L = −Δ`, the product rule gives `∇f·∇g = (f L g + g L f − L(fg))/2`. On a circle with `f = sin, g = cos`: v1 returns `+sin cos` (max|r + exact| = 3.7e-14), v2 returns `−sin cos` (max|r − exact| = 3.7e-14). v1's `laplacian` eigenvalues are ≥ 0, so this is not a sign-convention difference. It propagates into v1's `flexural_operator` variable-coefficient terms as DESIGN 21.7 states.

Concerns:
1. **No Sobolev-order guard** on `dirac`, `point_evaluation_operator`, `path_average_operator` (`base.py:645, 691, 1032`). On `L²` or `H^1` in 2D these silently produce representers that are grid-scale noise and adjoints that are numerically meaningless. v1 refused (`safe=True`).
2. **`Box.project_function` hard cutoff** (`box.py:190-204`) — see §1; a metric-independent but real change of numerical behaviour presented as a design decision.
3. **`pointwise_variance` uses `reference_point = origin` on boxes** (`fourier.py:298`). Because even axes carry a Nyquist cosine without its sine partner, `Σ_k φ_k(p)²` is *not* constant across the box (measured 0.986 of the mean at the origin for shape `(5,4,3)` — the origin is where every cosine is 1, so this is the *maximum*). The "provably the same everywhere" claim in `base.py:259` is false on a box with an even axis by a factor of order `1/n` per axis. Harmless on the sphere.
4. **Nyquist-derivative annihilation** in `derivative_operator` (`fourier.py:531-546`) is documented; its adjoint is consistent. Fine, but the codomain being the same Sobolev space (v1: order − 1) changes the operator norm and any bound built on it.
5. **`estimate_truncation_degree` orders by `degrees` then `searchsorted` on a cumulative sum** (`base.py:225-231`): `argsort` is not stable by default, so within a degree the cumulative sum reaches the threshold at an arbitrary component; the returned degree is still right, but pass `kind="stable"` or sum per degree.
6. **`Sphere.geodesic_quadrature` uses `arccos` for the angle** (`sphere.py:663`) immediately after the docstring on `geodesic_distance` (`sphere.py:636-642`) explains why `arccos` is the wrong formula. Nodes and weights for nearly coincident endpoints are affected.
7. **`PeriodicBox.evaluate`/`accumulate` unconditional `import finufft`** (`fourier.py:411, 452`) contradicts the documented fallback.
8. **`Sphere.spherical_cap_integral` on a Sobolev space** returns a `LinearFunctional` whose `.representer` is metric-correct (derivative components), but `geodesic_ball_average_operator` with `count=None` stacks `derivative_components` into `from_derivative_matrix` — correct. With `count` given it goes through the base quadrature path; the two agree (checked). No issue, noted only because it is the obvious place for the metric bug.
9. **Convention drift between doc and code.** DESIGN §13.4 says CS phase included; code excludes it. `sphere.py:1117` `Lebesgue(lmax, radius=)` vs DESIGN 20.1 examples using `(lat, lon)`.

## Recommendations

### Must
1. **Implement `geodesic_distance`, `geodesic_quadrature`, `walk_from`, `random_points` on `PeriodicBox` (and hence `Box`/`Interval`).** Periodic Euclidean distance with wrap on each axis (for `Box`, plain Euclidean in domain coordinates); straight-line Gauss-Legendre nodes; `walk_from` along axis 0. This unblocks `path_average_operator`, `geodesic_ball_average_operator`, `covariance_function` on boxes — which the catalogue marks Ported and which v1 had (`torus.py:347-373, 1118`; `plane.py:286-310, 646`).
2. **Restore the Sobolev-order guard.** In `SymmetricSpace.dirac`, `evaluate`, `accumulate`, `point_evaluation_operator`, `path_average_operator`, `geodesic_ball_average_operator`: if `self.order <= self.spatial_dimension / 2` raise `ValueError` (with an `unsafe=True` escape hatch), as v1 did at `symmetric_space.py:2448, 2467`.
3. **Fix the finufft thread default.** In `Sphere.evaluate/accumulate` (`sphere.py:487, 524`) and `PeriodicBox.evaluate/accumulate` (`fourier.py:430, 462`) pass `nthreads` explicitly (default 1, or `min(4, cores)`), and expose `eps`/`nthreads` on `point_evaluation_operator` and `path_average_operator`. Then re-measure the `_TRANSFORM_MIN_*` crossovers (`sphere.py:47-48`). Measured: 20 ms vs 170–800 ms at 2000 points.
4. **Replace `Sphere._quadrature` bootstrap with `pyshtools.utils.DHaj(self.grid_shape[0])`** (`sphere.py:358-382`); matches to 1.5e-16 and removes a 10 s (lmax 256) / minutes (lmax 512) first-call cost. Keep the current code as a test.
5. **Add `with_degree` and `degree_transfer_operator` to `PeriodicBox`/`Box`** (zero-pad/truncate the `rfftn` spectrum; adjoint via `from_derivative_callables` as on the sphere, `sphere.py:1033-1062`).
6. **Add public coefficient access.** `Sphere.to_coefficients(x) -> SHCoeffs` / `from_coefficients(coeffs)` and `PeriodicBox.to_coefficients(x) -> complex rfftn array` / `from_coefficients`, with the scaling documented (v2 components are `R ×` pyshtools' orthonormal coefficients). Also `from_coefficient_operator` (synthesis map Euclidean → space, v1 `sphere.py:882`) and a `power_spectrum(x)` helper to replace `sample_power_measure`.
7. **`pairs_within_distance`: use `scipy.spatial.cKDTree.sparse_distance_matrix` on chord vectors** as v1 did (`sphere.py:661-692`), keeping v2's diagonal handling. Move it and `cluster_points` to the base so boxes get them (they only need `geodesic_distance`/embedding).
8. **`power_measure`: replace the O(dim²) `counts` comprehension** (`base.py:585`) with `np.bincount(degrees)[degrees]`.
9. **`Box.project_function`: restore v1's raised-cosine taper** in the padding (`line.py:231-262`, `plane.py:156-201`) as the default, with `taper=False` for the hard cutoff, and document why.
10. **Provide public degree/lat-lon conversion** on `Sphere` (`from_latlon_degrees`, `to_latlon_degrees`, accepting arrays), and document the convention change in DESIGN.md for pyslfp.

### Should
11. Make the geometric primitives `@abstractmethod` on `SymmetricSpace` (`base.py:639, 998, 1004, 1017, 1132, 1138`) so a space cannot be constructed without them.
12. Cache `_packing`, `_legendre_indices`, `_quadrature` per `lmax` (module-level `lru_cache` keyed on `lmax`) rather than per instance, and vectorise `_legendre_indices` as `degrees*(degrees+1)//2 + orders`. Stops `with_order(0.0)` costing 0.2 s (lmax 256) on every `multiplication_operator`/`flexural_operator` call.
13. In `lift_formal_adjoint` (`base.py:1250-1262`), skip the `from_components(to_components(·))` round trip when `operator.domain`/`codomain` share the coordinate map with `domain`/`codomain` (same grid shape and `_key` ignoring order); document that callers must pass truncated inputs. Halves the transform count of `multiplication_operator` and cuts `flexural_operator` from 58 towards v1's 30.
14. `path_average_operator`: restore v1's `count=None` heuristic (`ceil(2·length/length_scale)`, `symmetric_space.py:2600`) and default `normalise=False` (integral) to match v1's documented semantics, or rename to make the change explicit.
15. `cluster_points`: restore `n_clusters=` and hierarchical linkage (`scipy.cluster.hierarchy.linkage` on the chord distance matrix), keeping the greedy method as an option.
16. Restore `CorrelatedInvariantGaussianMeasure` accessors as functions on the returned measure or on the space: `marginal(i)`, `cross_covariance(i, j)`, `spectral_correlations(i, j)`.
17. Restore `from_sobolev_parameters`-style `lmax` selection: a static `Sphere.truncation_degree_for(order, length_scale, radius, rtol)` that does not need a space (v1 `sphere.py:1141-1197`).
18. `estimate_truncation_degree`: sum power per degree (`np.bincount(degrees, weights=power)`) and search that, instead of an unstable `argsort`.
19. Use `atan2` in `Sphere.geodesic_quadrature` (`sphere.py:663`) as in `geodesic_distance`.
20. `PeriodicBox.evaluate`/`accumulate`: move `import finufft` after the `layout is None` check (`fourier.py:411-415, 452-456`).
21. `Box.support_projection`: delete the dead `traits` computation (`box.py:218-223`) or implement the lifted version instead of raising.
22. Fill the `Args`/`Returns`/units gaps listed in §4; in particular make every method that takes a radius say whether it is angular or physical, and every point argument say `(colatitude, longitude)` in radians.
23. Fix DESIGN §13.4 (Condon-Shortley phase is *excluded*) and §21.12 (`DHaj` *is* exposed).

### Consider
24. Offer `sampling=1` on `Sphere` (v1's default) to halve grid memory when longitude oversampling is not wanted; the transforms cost the same.
25. Vectorise `Sphere.project_function` (evaluate `f` on meshgrid arrays when it accepts arrays; fall back to the loop).
26. `derivative_operator` with codomain `with_order(order - 1)` (v1's choice) as an option, since that is the bounded map.
27. A public `Point`/`Points` helper type with `.colatitude/.longitude/.degrees()` so conventions live in one place; `plot_points`/`plot_paths` then accept either.
28. `pointwise_variance` on boxes: average `Σ φ_k(p)²` over the grid (one `basis_matrix` of the grid, or the exact `dim/V` when no Nyquist modes are present) instead of evaluating at the origin.
29. `Sphere.stations(include_names=True)`, and cache `_read_table` with `functools.lru_cache`.
30. Add a test that constructs every concrete space and calls `path_average_operator`, `covariance_function`, `with_degree`, `pairs_within_distance` on each — the absence of exactly this test is why the box regressions are invisible to the 1000-test suite.
